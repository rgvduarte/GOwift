## What counts as artwork

The image files in:

- `docs/assets/` (emblem, screenshots)
- `client/public/icons/` (app icons, favicons, brand mark)
- `landing/public/` and `landing/src/assets/` (landing page images and icons)
- `app/assets/` and the generated icons and splash screens under `app/android/`
  and `app/ios/`
- `brand/*.svg` (the logo sources)

The 3D world, riders and vehicles are drawn by code in `client/public/js/`
and fall under the AGPL like the rest of the code.

## What you may do

Use, copy, change and share the CC BY-SA artwork, also commercially and in forks, as
long as you:

- **credit it** as listed above ("Artwork © rgvduarte (GOindoor), CC BY-SA 4.0"
  or "Artwork © The OpenRadlr contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0"), with a link to the
  source repository, and say if you changed it;
- **share alike**: publish your changed versions under CC BY-SA 4.0 too.

## Name and logo

The licence covers the copyright in the files, not the **GOindoor name or
logo as a brand**. See [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md): a fork can reuse the
art, but must not present itself as GOindoor.
